Delete Your Corridor Account & Data

If you wish to delete your Corridor account and associated personal data, you can submit a request using the steps below.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to our support team at daniel.mamo@asteryagemstone.com from the email address registered to your Corridor account.
  2. Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request - Corridor".
  3. Include your registered username or phone number in the message body so we can locate your account.

Types of Data Deleted vs. Retained

  • Data Deleted: Your profile information (name, phone number, email), saved account credentials, and active personal preferences are permanently deleted from our active database upon request processing.
  • Data Retained: Certain transactional or support records may be retained for up to 24 months post-closure to comply with legal obligations, fraud prevention, or resolve any pending disputes, after which they are securely purged.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are typically processed within 5 to 7 business days. You will receive a confirmation email once your account and data have been successfully removed.
